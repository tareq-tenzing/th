02 · PAPERS & THESIS
Master’s thesis · Leuphana · 2025

Market Reactions to Voluntary Scope 3 Disclosure in Europe: The Moderating Role of Board-Level CSR/Sustainability Committees

Do investors reward European companies that voluntarily report their supply-chain (Scope 3) emissions, and does a board-level sustainability committee change that reaction? I prepared and checked firm-level market and disclosure data for European listed companies, then estimated abnormal returns and panel regressions in Stata.
